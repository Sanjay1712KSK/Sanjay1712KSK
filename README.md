## Hey, I'm Sanjay 👋

I'm a final-year **Computer Science and Engineering (AI & ML)** student at **SRM Institute of Science and Technology**, interested in building intelligent systems that connect machine learning research with practical engineering.

My work spans **computer vision, reinforcement learning, robotics, simulation, local AI systems and full-stack product development**.

- 🤖 Exploring learning-based control, autonomous systems and simulation-driven robotics.
- 👁️ Building computer-vision systems for detection, tracking and visual understanding.
- 🧠 Interested in local-first AI, LLM applications, RAG and intelligent developer tools.
- 🛠️ Working across model development, backend systems, desktop applications and product interfaces.
- 🚀 Building independent software projects under **[Ayan Crafts](https://github.com/Ayan-Crafts)**.

> **My engineering philosophy:** Build systems that learn, not just run.
